# Hipster's Respawn Penalty

Dying has a cost: you feel weaker, your gear is fried, and you can't go back to where you died so easily anymore. Get a nice sleep and regroup. Or craft a Holy Flask. That works too.

You ever die somewhere that was just too hard, stare at the empty hotbar, and realize the save doesn't mean much without that kit? You have to walk back in empty-handed to the exact place that killed you, because that's where the pile is. Keep inventory fixes that. You keep the stuff. Then the place isn't hard anymore - you can run it forever, because dying costs nothing. That's the hole this mod fills. Penalties. A danger zone on the death location. You start getting that *meh, that's too dangerous for now* feeling. Finally.

Keep inventory is a good pairing, not a requirement. With it on, the penalty *is* the cost. With it off, you still drop your stuff and the death zone is sitting on the pile. Either way, you don't get to pretend the death didn't happen.

## After you die

You come back worse. Less health. Less hunger. The more you die in a short window, the worse it gets - up to a point. If you get a good night's sleep, you start over like a reasonable person.

Your gear **Frays** while that penalty is on. Held tools and worn armor chew through durability faster. You'll see a red foil on them that warns you.

## The death zone

The place you died stays hostile for a while. Rush back in and you'll meet your end: stronger debuffs, weaker swings. Walk out and that extra pressure drops. The Fraying does not. That follows you home.

If you died basically on top of your bed, you get a short protected window so you wont get spawnkilled.

## Getting better

Sleep through the night. Or just survive a Minecraft day. Each time, the penalty eases one level.

At the high end, the bed might ignore you for a few minutes. That's on purpose. Die-sleep-reset should not be a strategy.

When you're finally clear, Fraying hangs around for a couple of minutes. You can also craft a cure. A Holy Flask skips the lecture and wipes everything.

## Configure it as you like

Tune it in Mod Menu, or in `config/hipsters_respawn_penalty.json`.
